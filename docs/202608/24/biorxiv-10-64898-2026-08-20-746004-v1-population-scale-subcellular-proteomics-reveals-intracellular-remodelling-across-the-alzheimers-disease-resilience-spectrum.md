## 摘要
人类组织的全蛋白质组分析已改变了我们对疾病的理解，但所提供的关于蛋白质定位——蛋白质组中一个功能信息丰富的维度——的见解有限。在阿尔茨海默病中，淀粉样蛋白-β和tau表现出异常的定位，然而空间重排是否扩展到全蛋白质组水平，对于基于丰度的蛋白质组学而言仍然是无法触及的。在此，我们开发了比较性亚细胞蛋白质组学，应用于来自跨越阿尔茨海默病-韧性谱系的75个个体的背外侧前额叶皮层，对疾病中蛋白质定位进行建模。我们识别出217个与疾病相关的定位变化，这些变化富集于内溶酶体功能、细胞内运输和RNA加工，并解析了不溶性聚集体中的tau蛋白形式。我们最强的定位候选物在全组织丰度上仅显示出适度的差异，突出了常规蛋白质组学无法触及的疾病生物学。我们验证了CSNK1A1与病理性tau的共定位，并识别了SCAI（一种此前在人类大脑中未被表征的癌症相关蛋白）的一个意外神经元定位模式，凸显了亚细胞蛋白质组学在组织中的发现潜力。

## Abstract
Proteome-wide analyses of human tissue have transformed our understanding of disease, but provide limited insight into protein localisation, a functionally informative dimension of the proteome. In Alzheimer's disease, amyloid-{beta} and tau exhibit aberrant localisation, yet whether spatial reorganisation extends proteome-wide has remained inaccessible to abundance-based proteomics. Here, we develop comparative subcellular proteomics applied to dorsolateral prefrontal cortex from 75 individuals spanning the Alzheimer's disease-resilience spectrum, modelling protein localisation across disease. We identify 217 disease-associated localisation shifts enriched for endolysosomal function, intracellular trafficking, and RNA processing, and resolve tau proteoforms within insoluble aggregates. Our strongest localisation candidates show only modest differences in whole-tissue abundance, highlighting disease biology inaccessible to conventional proteomics. We validate co-localisation of CSNK1A1 with pathological tau and identify an unexpected neuronal localisation pattern for SCAI, a cancer-associated protein not previously characterised in human brain, highlighting the discovery potential of subcellular proteomics in tissue.
